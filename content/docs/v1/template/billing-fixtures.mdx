# Local billing fixtures

The local catalog contains two non-chargeable products: `fixture-monthly` and
`fixture-pack`. Their sample amounts exist only to exercise request validation,
checkout attempts, signed local fixture outcomes, webhook inbox processing,
reconciliation and credit ledger behavior. The production catalog is empty
until a separately reviewed payment adapter and account profile are configured.

Open the workspace Billing page and select a fixture product. The checkout page
can complete or fail the fixture, and the portal handoff remains on the
application origin. A completed fixture event does not represent a Stripe
payment. Duplicate event delivery should converge on the same business effects;
refund and dispute outcomes are represented as recovery events and retain their
own ledger identities.

The authenticated checkout route accepts only a catalog product key. The server
owns customer identity, amount, currency, price reference and checkout URL. Do
not add provider identifiers or a browser-selected return URL to the request.
See the [API contract examples](../api/overview) and the
[billing recovery operations](./operations).

## Isolated Stripe test acceptance

`BILLING_DRIVER=stripe_test` activates the test adapter only with
`NODE_ENV=production`, `SHPTITTO_RUNTIME_PROFILE=acceptance`, a test secret key,
an HTTPS `APP_URL`, the endpoint's `BILLING_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, and an explicit
`STRIPE_PORTAL_CONFIGURATION_ID`. Configure an isolated migrated database and
buyer-owned Stripe sandbox. An installed Stripe key by itself enables nothing.

`STRIPE_TEST_CATALOG_JSON` contains server-owned, validated catalog snapshots.
Each snapshot binds one monthly subscription or permanent credit pack to its
Stripe test Price, currency, integer cash amount, integer credit quantity and
snapshot hash. Use the catalog contract to construct and validate those terms;
do not copy browser-supplied prices or infer credits from a payment amount.
These test terms are not approved commercial pricing.

The owner Billing page opens hosted Stripe Checkout and the configured customer
portal. Disable subscription updates, schedule cancellation at period end and
set cancellation proration to `none`. Returning to the application does not
grant credits. Only signed test events followed by authoritative provider
readback and fenced database reconciliation can change the ledger.

Checkout and customer retries keep their durable provider keys. Unknown outcomes
retain their purchase slot and require reconciliation after the conservative
23-hour retry window. Refunds are operator/domain commands; pending or unknown
refunds cannot confirm cash or revoke credits. Verify subscription, pack,
webhook replay, refund and owner/member behavior before closing acceptance.
Commercial production billing and real email delivery require separate release
configuration and evidence.
