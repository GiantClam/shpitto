# Marketing Landing Site

Use this skill when the site is primarily conversion-driven and centered on one product, offer, or campaign.

## Core contract

- Optimize for one dominant conversion path.
- Route structure should remain intentionally small.
- Homepage may use stronger marketing rhythm than corporate sites, but still must remain coherent and proof-led.
- Avoid importing enterprise multi-page information architecture by default.
